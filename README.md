An interactive sales analytics dashboard built in **Microsoft Excel** to analyze makeup product sales across 8 countries, 8 brands, and multiple sales channels. The project transforms raw transactional data into clear, visual insights using Pivot Tables, Charts, Slicers, and Timeline filters.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes **500 makeup sales transactions** from January to December 2025, covering brands like MAC, L'Oreal, Dior, and Fenty Beauty. Three interactive dashboards were built to answer key business questions around revenue, product performance, and sales behavior.

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| 🗓️ Time Period | January 2025 – December 2025 |
| 💰 Total Revenue | $793,188.50 USD |
| 📦 Total Records | 500 Transactions |
| 🌍 Countries | 8 |
| 🏷️ Brands | 8 |
| 🧴 Product Types | 8 |

## 📋 Dataset Description

The raw dataset contains the following columns:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| `Sale_ID` | Unique ID for each transaction |
| `Date` | Date of the sale |
| `Brand` | Makeup brand name |
| `Product_Type` | Product category (e.g., Mascara, Lipstick) |
| `Country` | Country where the sale occurred |
| `Sales_Channel` | How it was sold (Online, Retail Store, Mall, Beauty Salon) |
| `Payment_Method` | Payment used (Card, Cash, Digital Wallet) |
| `Price_USD` | Price per unit in USD |
| `Units_Sold` | Number of units sold |
| `Revenue_USD` | Total revenue = Price × Units Sold |

## 📈 Dashboards

### 1️⃣ Revenue Dashboard
- **Revenue by Country** — bar chart comparing all 8 countries
- **Slicers** — filter by Country and Sales Channel
- Quickly identifies top and low-performing markets

### 2️⃣ Product Analysis Dashboard
- **Top Selling Products** — ranked by total revenue
- **Top Performing Brands** — MAC leads with $155,246
- **Sales by Product Category** — compare all 8 product types
- **Product Contribution** — pie chart showing revenue share
- **Timeline Filter** — explore data month by month
- **Slicers** — filter by brand, country, and channel

### 3️⃣ Sales Performance Dashboard
- **Revenue by Payment Method** — Digital Wallet, Card, Cash
- **Revenue by Country** — geographic performance view
- **Units Sold by Payment Method** — volume vs value analysis
- **Units Sold by Brand** — brand volume comparison

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 🏆 Top Performers
| Category | Winner | Revenue |
|---|---|---|
| 🏷️ Brand | MAC | $155,246 |
| 🧴 Product | Mascara | $116,135 |
| 🌍 Country | Saudi Arabia | $120,778 |
| 🛒 Channel | Online | $224,883 |
| 💳 Payment | Digital Wallet | $290,528 |

### 📌 Notable Findings
- **Eye products dominate** — Mascara, Eyeshadow, and Eyeliner are the top 3 categories
- **Online sales lead** all channels, reflecting a shift toward digital shopping
- **Digital Wallet** is the most preferred payment method
- **Saudi Arabia and UK** are the strongest geographic markets
- **Lipstick and Maybelline** are underperforming and need strategic attention

## 💡 Recommendations

1. **Boost eye product inventory** — Mascara, Eyeshadow, and Eyeliner drive the most revenue
2. **Invest in Online channel** — it already leads and has the most growth potential
3. **Expand in Saudi Arabia and UK** — the strongest geographic markets
4. **Improve Lipstick sales** — bundle offers or influencer campaigns could help
5. **Support Digital Wallet payments** across all channels — most customers prefer it
6. **Focus marketing on MAC and L'Oreal** — they generate the highest brand revenue

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download the file `Makep_Dashboard.xlsx`
2. Open it in **Microsoft Excel** (2016 or later recommended)
3. Go to the **Dashboard**, **Product Analysis**, or **Sales Performance Analysis** sheet
4. Use the **Slicers** to filter by country, brand, or channel
5. Use the **Timeline** to explore data by month or quarter
6. Visit the **Insights & Recommendations** sheet for a summary of key findings
